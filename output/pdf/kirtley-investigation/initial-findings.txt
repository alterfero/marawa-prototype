A star identifies a Kirtley addition but does not guarantee an unused TMI number.
The complete 22-code candidate list and matched record starts are supplied.

BOUNDARY OF THIS REVIEW
Eight non-plus repeated-label groups remained after allowing internal spacing;
seven have no unresolved graphic-marker issue in the reviewed pages, while
J2048 requires the visual qualification above. Seventy-four additional repeated
labels contain '+'. All are retained in the candidate queue.

The candidate scan does not cover every possible semantic mismatch. It has not
validated all entry boundaries, all bibliographic identities, all page locators,
or the cited narratives themselves. A full investigation must preserve explicit
coverage and uncertainty, rather than present these findings as all errors.
